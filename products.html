PLANNED AREAS — NOT A LIVE PRODUCT CATALOG

Four connected areas of exploration.

These are intended areas of focus. No product should be considered available unless its own release details explicitly say so.

AREA / 01

Atomic AI

Potential AI-assisted workflows for coding, research, writing, document analysis, and practical automation. The first product concept will be chosen after customer validation.

AREA / 02

Atomic Software

Web platforms, developer tools, and SaaS products designed to solve well-defined user and business problems.

AREA / 03

Atomic Research

Long-term work in scientific computing, model evaluation, reproducible experiments, and carefully documented technical investigations.

AREA / 04

Atomic Systems

Future exploration of robotics and embedded technology when a validated use case, technical capacity, and suitable budget exist.

How we choose what to build

  • Talk to users and validate the problem before committing to a build.
  • Define a small first version with clear acceptance criteria.
  • Measure usefulness, reliability, operating cost, and ongoing demand.
  • Protect customer data and keep credentials out of source control.
  • Expand only when the product or research evidence justifies the next step.

Initial product hypothesis

A unified AI workspace is one possible starting point—not a launched product or finalized decision.

The idea would be to make selected AI-assisted tasks easier from one place. Before building it, we need to validate which tasks users value most, the cost of serving them, provider terms, and whether the experience can be meaningfully differentiated.
