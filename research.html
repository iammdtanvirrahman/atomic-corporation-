Our long-term goal is to become a research-capable technology company. We plan to earn that capability with focused questions, reproducible experiments, and honest reporting.

RESEARCH PROGRAMME IN PLANNING

Research begins with a good question.

We do not claim to operate a full research lab today. This page outlines the approach we intend to develop as the company gains technical capacity and resources.

TRACK / 01

AI evaluation

Explore reliable methods to evaluate AI systems, compare results, and understand limits across practical tasks.

TRACK / 02

Scientific computing

Investigate computational methods and small simulations with clear assumptions and reproducible results.

TRACK / 03

Engineering systems

Build the foundations for future experiments involving embedded systems, robotics, and intelligent hardware.

TRACK / 04

Open technical work

Where suitable, share code, technical notes, benchmarks, and methods so others can inspect and reproduce findings.

Research standards

  • Define the hypothesis, method, inputs, and evaluation criteria before interpreting results.
  • Separate measured results from estimates, opinions, and plans.
  • Record enough context to reproduce experiments, subject to privacy and licensing constraints.
  • Report relevant limitations and negative results rather than hiding them.
  • Respect dataset licenses, model terms, privacy, safety, and intellectual property.

How this becomes a real programme

Our planned path is to start with small, low-cost experiments; develop evaluation and documentation habits; seek mentorship and collaboration; and only then consider substantial compute spending or larger model-training efforts.

Research promise: We will describe actual research outputs when they exist. Planned topics on this page are not published findings, completed experiments, or claims of scientific breakthrough.
